Press Start. You get 10 seconds to get in place.

Next: Chair push-up

Form tips
  1. Set up. Feet shoulder-width apart, toes pointing slightly out, arms by your sides.
  2. Move. Breathe in as you push your hips back and bend your knees until your thighs are level with the floor. Bring your hands together at your chest. Hold for 1 second, then breathe out and stand up.
  3. Hold the shape. Chest up, heels flat, knees pointing the same way as your toes.

Too easy? Take 3 seconds on the way down.

Knees complain? Go a little less deep.
