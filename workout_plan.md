# My 12-Week Home Workout Plan

**Why I'm doing this:** discipline (show up every day), feel good and confident, more energy, a calmer mind, better focus.
**Equipment:** 2 × 5 kg dumbbells, a sturdy chair with no wheels (push it against a wall), a mat.
**Limits:** quiet moves only (no jumping), about 23–30 minutes a day, 7 days a week with 2 easy days.

---

## The 5 rules

1. **Do something every day:** the full session, or the 10-minute minimum version (see below).
2. **Never miss two days in a row.**
3. **Don't count reps.** Move at a steady pace until the timer beeps.
4. **Effort check:**
   - Strength moves: the last few seconds should feel hard, but your form stays clean.
   - Cardio: you're breathing hard but could still say a short sentence.
5. **Safety:**
   - Sharp pain or joint pain: stop that move and use the easier version.
   - Dizziness or chest pain: stop the session.
   - Normal muscle soreness in the first days is fine. Keep going.

## How the 12 weeks get harder

Every 2 weeks, exactly **one** thing changes.

| Weeks | Phase | Timer (work / rest) | Rounds | Main block | Full session |
|-------|-------|---------------------|--------|------------|--------------|
| 1–2 | Start | 30 s / 30 s | 2 | 13 min | ~23 min |
| 3–4 | Start | 40 s / 20 s | 2 | 13 min | ~23 min |
| 5–6 | Build (harder moves) | 40 s / 20 s | 2 | 13 min | ~23 min |
| 7–8 | Build | 40 s / 20 s | 3 | 20 min | ~30 min |
| 9–10 | Grow (hardest moves) | 40 s / 20 s | 3 | 20 min | ~30 min |
| 11–12 | Grow | 45 s / 15 s | 3 | 20 min | ~30 min |

- Each exercise slot is exactly 1 minute (work + rest).
- One round is 6 exercises, so 6 minutes.
- There is a 1-minute break between rounds.

The first 6 weeks are kept short (~23 min) on purpose, so building the daily habit comes first.

---

## Every session has the same shape

1. **Warm-up:** 5 min
2. **Main block:** 13–20 min
3. **Cool-down:** 5 min (stretch 2 min + breathing 3 min)

### Warm-up (every day: 5 moves × 1 min, no rest)
1. March in place, swinging your arms
2. Arm circles: forward 30 s, backward 30 s
3. Hip circles: 30 s each direction
4. Slow bodyweight squats
5. Reach up tall, then hinge forward and reach toward your shins

### Cool-down (strength and cardio days)
1. Chest opener: clasp your hands behind your back and lift your chest (40 s)
2. Hamstring stretch: one heel forward, hinge at the hips, switch sides at the halfway beep (40 s)
3. Child's pose on the mat (40 s)
4. Breathing: in through the nose for 4 s, out slowly for 6 s (3 min)

| # | Exercise | Weeks 1–4 | Weeks 5–8 | Weeks 9–12 |
|---|----------|-----------|-----------|------------|
| 1 | Squat | Bodyweight squat, 1 s hold at the bottom | Goblet squat (1 dumbbell at chest), 1 s hold | Goblet squat, 3 s down + 1 s hold |
| 2 | Push-up | Chair push-up (hands on seat) | Knee push-up | Full push-ups; drop to knees when tired |
| 3 | Row | Bent-over row, both dumbbells | Same + squeeze 1 s at the top | Same + 3 s lowering |
| 4 | Glute bridge | Bodyweight | 1 dumbbell resting on hips | Single-leg (switch at half) |
| 5 | Shoulder press | Seated on chair | Standing | Standing, 3 s lowering |
| 6 | Core | Heel taps | Dead bug | Dead bug, slow 3 s reach |

|---|----------|-----------|-----------|------------|

## Quiet Cardio (Tue, Thu)

Same 6 moves throughout; they get faster and a bit harder each phase. None of them involve jumping.

| # | Exercise | Weeks 1–4 | Weeks 5–8 | Weeks 9–12 |
|---|----------|-----------|-----------|------------|
| 1 | High-knee march | Steady march | Fast march | Quick high knees, soft feet |
| 2 | Step-jacks | Steady | Brisk | Fast |
| 3 | Squat + calf raise | Squat, then rise onto toes | Add an overhead reach | Add a 1 s hold on toes |
| 4 | Shadow boxing | Jab-cross | Jab-cross-uppercut | Combos + quick duck (mini squat) |
| 5 | Cross-body knee to elbow | Steady | Brisk | Fast |
| 6 | Step-back lunge + reach | Half depth | Full depth | Add a knee drive as you come up |

## Saturday: Core + Stretch (easy, ~24 min)

1. **Warm-up:** 5 min (same as always)
2. **Core:** 5 moves, 40 s / 20 s, 2 rounds, 1 min break (11 min)
   1. Bird dog (switch side at half)
   2. Kneeling heel touches (alternate)
   3. Side plank on knees (switch at half); full side plank in weeks 9–12
   4. Glute bridge hold
   5. Kneeling side bend (alternate)
3. **Stretch:** 5 × 1 min. Cat-cow, kneeling hip-flexor stretch (switch at half), open book (switch at half), child's pose, seated hamstring stretch.
4. **Breathing:** 3 min

## Sunday: Walk + Stretch + Breathe (easy, ~20 min)

1. **Walk** around your home at an easy pace: 12 min (music, a podcast or audio notes are fine)
2. **Stretch:** 5 × 1 min. Chest opener, hamstring stretch, hip-flexor stretch (switch at half), neck and shoulder rolls, child's pose.
3. **Breathing:** 3 min

### Test Sundays (weeks 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12)

Before the walk, do the warm-up (5 min), then:
1. **Push-ups in 60 s:** full push-ups, chest close to the floor
2. Rest 2 min
3. **Squats in 60 s:** thighs level with the floor on each rep
4. Rest 2 min
5. **Plank:** hold as long as you can with a straight body

Write down the three numbers. On test days the walk is 6 min (~27 min total).

- **Starting point:** 1 push-up, about 20 squats in a row.
- **Rough targets for week 12** (it's fine if you're below these): 5–10 push-ups, 30+ squats in 60 s, 60 s plank.

---

## Minimum day (~10 min), for bad days

- **Mon–Fri:** 2 min warm-up (march + arm circles), then 1 round of today's main block (6 min), then 2 min breathing.
- **Sat–Sun:** 8 min walk, then 2 min breathing.

It counts, and your streak continues.

## Exercise guide

**Strength**
- **Bodyweight squat:** feet shoulder-width apart, toes slightly out; push your hips back and bend your knees until your thighs are level with the floor, hands together at your chest; hold 1 s, then stand up. Breathe in on the way down, out on the way up.
- **Goblet squat:** same, holding one dumbbell upright against your chest.
- **Chair push-up:** hands on the chair seat (chair against a wall), body straight; lower your chest to the seat edge and push back.
- **Knee push-up:** on the floor with knees down; body straight from knees to head.
- **Full push-up:** on hands and toes, body straight; chest close to the floor, then push up.
- **Bent-over row:** a dumbbell in each hand, hinge forward to about 45° with a flat back; pull the dumbbells to your ribs.
- **One-arm row:** one hand on the chair seat, opposite foot back; pull the dumbbell to your hip; switch at half.
- **Glute bridge:** lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat; push your hips up, squeeze, lower.
- **Single-leg glute bridge:** same with one leg straight; switch at half.
- **Shoulder press:** dumbbells at your shoulders, press them overhead, lower. When standing, keep your ribs down and don't arch your back.
- **Heel taps:** lie on your back, knees bent at 90° in the air; slowly lower one heel to tap the floor, return, alternate.
- **Dead bug:** lie on your back, arms up, knees at 90°; slowly extend the opposite arm and leg toward the floor, return, alternate. Keep your lower back on the mat.
- **Romanian deadlift (RDL):** dumbbells in front of your thighs, soft knees; push your hips back and slide the dumbbells down to your shins with a flat back; squeeze your glutes to stand.
- **Single-leg RDL:** one hand on the chair, dumbbell in the other hand; hinge on one leg as the other leg reaches back; switch at half.
- **Split squat:** one foot forward, one back, holding the chair; lower your back knee toward the floor, rise; switch at half.
- **Reverse lunge:** step one foot back, lower, step back to standing; alternate.
- **Hammer curl:** palms facing each other, elbows by your sides; curl up, lower slowly. *(Your Forearms video #1)*
- **Reverse curl:** palms facing down, elbows by your sides; curl up, lower slowly. *(Your Forearms video #3)*
- **Plank:** forearms on the mat, body straight. Knees down for the easier version.
- **Plank shoulder taps:** plank on your hands; tap the opposite shoulder, keeping your hips steady.

**Cardio**
- **High-knee march:** march in place, knees to hip height, pump your arms.
- **Step-jacks:** a quiet jumping jack. Step one foot out to the side as your arms go up, return, then the other side.
- **Squat + calf raise:** squat, stand, then rise onto your toes.
- **Shadow boxing:** boxing stance, throw punches with light feet and no stomping. *(Your "instead of running" video)*
- **Cross-body knee to elbow:** hands behind your head; bring one knee up to the opposite elbow, alternate. *(Your "instead of running" and chair videos)*
- **Step-back lunge + reach:** step back into a lunge as both arms reach overhead; alternate.

**Core and stretch**
- **Bird dog:** on hands and knees, extend the opposite arm and leg, hold 2 s; switch at half.
- **Kneeling heel touches:** tall kneel; reach one hand back to the same-side heel with the other arm up; alternate. *(Your Hindi kneeling video)*
- **Side plank on knees:** lie on your side, forearm under your shoulder, knees bent; lift your hips; switch at half.
- **Glute bridge hold:** bridge up and hold.
- **Kneeling side bend:** tall kneel; slide one hand down the side of your thigh while the other arm reaches over; alternate. *(Your Hindi kneeling video)*
- **Cat-cow:** on hands and knees, slowly round your back, then let it sag gently.
- **Kneeling hip-flexor stretch:** one knee down, the other foot forward; gently push your hips forward.
- **Open book:** lie on your side with knees bent and arms together in front; open the top arm to the other side; switch at half.
- **Child's pose:** knees down, sit back on your heels, arms forward, forehead down.
