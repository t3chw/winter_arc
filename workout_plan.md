# My 12-Week Home Workout Plan

**Why I'm doing this:** discipline (show up every day), feel good and confident, more energy, a calmer mind, better focus.
**Equipment:** 2 × 5 kg dumbbells, a sturdy chair with no wheels (push it against a wall), a mat.
**Limits:** quiet moves only (no jumping), about 30 minutes a day, 7 days a week with 2 easier days.

**How to use it:** open the site, press **Start** on today's card, put your music on and copy the figure. The whole day runs on its own (warm-up, main part, finisher, cool-down) with no stops and nothing to decide. The next day unlocks when you finish.

---

## The 5 rules

1. **Do something every day:** the full session, or the 9-minute version on bad days.
2. **Never miss two days in a row.**
3. **Don't count reps.** Keep moving until the timer ends.
4. **Effort:**
   - Main part: the last 10 seconds of every move should feel very hard, but your form stays clean.
   - Finisher: all out for 20 seconds. You should be breathing too hard to talk.
5. **Safety:**
   - Sharp pain or joint pain: stop that move and use the easier version (form tips on the site).
   - Dizziness or chest pain: stop the session.
   - Normal muscle soreness in the first week is expected. Keep going.

---

## The 7-day cycle (repeats for 12 weeks)

Days go in order. Day 2 comes after you finish day 1, whatever the weekday.

| Day | Session | Minutes | Feel |
|-----|---------|---------|------|
| 1 | Strength A: dumbbells, legs + push | 31 | Very hard |
| 2 | Quiet HIIT | 30 | Very hard |
| 3 | Strength B: dumbbells, hinge + pull | 31 | Very hard |
| 4 | Plank + cardio burn | 31 | Very hard |
| 5 | Full-body dumbbells | 30 | Very hard |
| 6 | Core + stretch | 27 | Easier |
| 7 | Reset: walk + stretch + breathe (test every 2nd week) | 26–28 | Easy |

## How the 12 weeks get harder

The minutes stay the same; the rest gets shorter.

| Weeks | Work / rest |
|-------|-------------|
| 1–2 | 40 s / 20 s |
| 3–6 | 45 s / 15 s |
| 7–12 | 50 s / 10 s |

Finishers are always 20 s all out / 10 s rest. There is a 1-minute water break after every round.

---

## Every hard day has the same shape

1. **Warm-up, 4 min** (40 s each): march in place, arm circles, hip circles, slow squats, walk-out to plank.
2. **Main part, 20 min.**
3. **Finisher, 4–5 min,** all out.
4. **Cool-down, 3 min:** chest opener, hamstring stretch, child's pose (40 s each), then 1 min of slow breathing (4 s in, 6 s out).

### Day 1: Strength A (3 rounds)
Goblet squat · chair push-up · alternating row · reverse lunge + curl · seated shoulder press · up-down plank.
**Finisher (8 sets):** dumbbell thrusters, then mountain climbers, alternating.

### Day 2: Quiet HIIT (4 blocks, each done once)
1. Step-jacks · squat + knee to elbow · step-back burpee · cross-body knee to elbow · superman pull
2. High-knee march · squat + calf raise · front kick toe touch · in & out quick steps · glute bridge
3. Rope skip (no hop) · reverse lunge + kick · walk-out to plank · standing side crunch · heel touches
4. Skater steps · plank shoulder taps · squat pulses · mountain climbers · donkey kicks

### Day 3: Strength B (3 rounds)
Romanian deadlift · dumbbell split squat · dumbbell floor press · one-arm row · side lunge + lateral raise · leg raise + dumbbell hold.
**Finisher (8 sets):** dumbbell swings, then step-back burpees, alternating.

### Day 4: Plank + cardio burn (3 rounds)
Plank shoulder taps · squat + knee to elbow · up-down plank · skater steps · flutter kicks · high-knee march.
Getting down to the floor and back up every minute is what makes this one hard.
**Finisher (8 sets):** step-back burpees, then mountain climbers, alternating.

### Day 5: Full-body dumbbells (2 rounds of 8)
Sumo squat + curl · renegade row (knees down) · deadlift + squat · overhead triceps extension · curl to press · bridge hold + chest press · push-up + climbers · side plank with arm up.
**Finisher (10 sets):** squat pulses, then in & out quick steps, alternating.

### Day 6: Core + stretch (easier)
1. Warm-up, 4 min
2. **Core, 2 rounds at 40 s / 20 s:** bird dog · superman · side plank on knees · heel bridge · seated cross punches · knee plank
3. **Stretch, 1 min each:** cat-cow · hip-flexor stretch · cobra · butterfly · lying hamstring stretch · seated glute stretch · child's pose
4. Breathing, 2 min

### Day 7: Reset (easy)
1. **Walk** around your home, 15 min (music or audio notes are fine)
2. **Stretch, 1 min each:** chest opener · standing side bend · hamstring stretch · hip-flexor stretch · neck and shoulder rolls · knee hug · child's pose
3. **Breathing,** 3 min

**Test every 2nd week (days 14, 28, 42…):** warm-up, then push-ups in 60 s, squats in 60 s, and a plank held as long as you can, with 1 min rest between. The walk is 8 min that day. Write the three numbers down.
- **Starting point:** 1 push-up, about 20 squats.
- **Rough targets for week 12:** 5–10 push-ups, 30+ squats in 60 s, 60 s plank.

## Bad day? The 9-minute version

The link under the Start button: 2 warm-up moves, one round of today's moves, 1 min of breathing. On days 6 and 7 it is a 5-minute walk instead. It counts, and your streak continues.

## Exercise guide

Every move has its own moving guide on the site (Exercise library), with form tips and an easier or harder version.
The moves come from these videos, changed to quiet versions with no jumping:
- **30-minute dumbbell workout:** sumo squat + curl, alternating row, deadlift + squat, overhead triceps extension, side lunge + lateral raise, curl to press, leg raise + dumbbell, bridge + chest press, side plank, push-up + climbers, renegade row, reverse lunge + curl; warm-up walk-out; cool-down knee hug, lying hamstring, butterfly, seated glute stretch.
- **25-minute HIIT (5 rounds):**
  - Jumping jacks became step-jacks. Squat knee to elbow and back extension pull became the superman pull.
  - A-skips and jogging became the high-knee march. Jump rope became the rope skip, on your toes with no hop.
  - Bounce squats became squat pulses. Also from this video: kick backs, plank arm walk, heel touches, heel bridge.
- **20-minute HIIT:**
  - High knees became the march. Lateral jacks and squat jacks became in & out steps and side-step squats.
  - Skaters became skater steps with no jump. Plyo and scissor lunges became the reverse lunge + kick.
  - Also from this video: shoulder taps, flutter kicks, sitting cross punches, mountain climbers.
  - Plank jacks were left out: mountain climbers and shoulder taps cover the same work.

Retest, then start a new 12-week plan. Your legs will likely need more than 5 kg by then, so we'll decide about heavier dumbbells at that point.
